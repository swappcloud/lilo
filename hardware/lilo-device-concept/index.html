A rounded enclosure with an integral foot, snap-fit covers, a round AMOLED display, a Raspberry Pi and USB audio. The power button and microphone mute have their own physical connections.

Selected hardware

  1. Printed body, front, bezel and removable rear
  2. Waveshare ESP32-S3 AMOLED 1.75 and its controller
  3. Raspberry Pi 4 Model B and sliding carrier
  4. Seeed ReSpeaker Lite USB board, with a printed flexure pressing its real mute switch
  5. Seeed 114993346 enclosed speaker and support
  6. Momentary GPIO3 power button, native USB-C power entry and actual cable routes

The display and audio board use manufacturer STEP geometry. The Pi and speaker are dimensioned reconstructions. Cable curves show routing space, not a tested harness.

Print and assemble

The covers use inward-release snap tabs; the body has a flat contact patch instead of a separate stand. The display retaining clips and carrier can be removed for service.

Printed joints use snap or sliding fits. The selected panel button and connector retain their supplied nuts; the Pi uses its standard board fasteners. Print a fit coupon and check each purchased revision before the final assembly.
